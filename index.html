How a community foundation works

Three steps, one shared endowment

  1. Step 1

    Donors give

    Individuals, families, and businesses contribute to a pooled, permanent endowment.

  2. Step 2

    We invest and grow

    Funds are professionally invested and sustained for generations, not just this year.

  3. Step 3

    Grants go out

    The foundation grants to nonprofits and community projects across Greater Cleveland every year.
